### Domain A: Yogic Mobility, Patañjali Siddhis & The Quantum Conflation
* **Canonical Citation**:
  * *Yoga Sūtra 3.42*: कायाकाशयोः सम्बन्धसंयमाल्लघुतूलसमापत्तेश्चाकाशगमनम्॥
  * *Yoga Sūtra 3.48*: ततो मनोजवित्वं विकरणभावः प्रधानजयश्च॥
  * *Yoga Sūtra 3.37 (Critical Boundary)*: ते समाधावुपसर्गा व्युत्थाने सिद्धयः॥
* **Scriptural & Philosophical Truth**:
  * "Manojavitva" (speed of the mind) in classical Yoga represents sensory transcendence and uninhibited cognitive mastery (*Pradhāna-jaya*), not macroscopic biological teleportation.
  * Patañjali expressly warns in YS 3.37 that Siddhis are *Upasargas* (obstacles, worldly distractions) to true Samādhi and Kaivalya.
  * In the Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa, Paraśurāma's swiftness to Kailāśa is described through high poetic idiom (*Manojavena*), while his pedestrian return upon Jamadagni's martyrdom honors historical, physical causality and biological grief within earthly Dharma-Kṣetra.
* **The Quantum Optical Illusion Deconstructed**:
  * Quantum teleportation (Bennett et al. 1993) transfers quantum state information between subatomic particles via entangled Bell pairs and a classical sub-lightspeed communication channel.
  * Projecting quantum teleportation onto human biological bodies ($10^{28}$ atoms) is a category error and a modern pseudoscientific conflation.
