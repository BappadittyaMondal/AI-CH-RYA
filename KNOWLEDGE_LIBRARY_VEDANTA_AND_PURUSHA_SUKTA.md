## 2. Dossier 01: Ātmabodha — Systematic Advaita Vedānta Pedagogy

### 2.1 Core Philosophical Architecture
*Ātmabodha* ("Self-Knowledge") is designed for aspirants whose minds are tranquil (*Śānta*), free from worldly craving, and seeking direct experiential realization (*Aparokṣa-Anubhūti*).

1. **Jñāna as the Exclusive Direct Means to Mokṣa (Verses 2–3)**:
   - *Text*: बोधोऽन्यसाधनेभ्यो हि साक्षात् मोक्षैकसाधनम्। पाकस्य वह्निवत् ज्ञानं विना मोक्षो न सिध्यति॥ (v. 2)
   - *Epistemic Principle*: Just as fire is the direct, indispensable cause of cooking (wood, water, and pot are auxiliary), Knowledge (*Jñāna*) alone directly yields Liberation (*Mokṣa*).
   - *Khaṇḍana of Karma-vāda (v. 3)*: अविरोधितया कर्म नाविद्यां विनिवर्तयेत्। विद्याऽविद्यां निहन्त्येव तेजस्तिमिरसङ्घवत्॥  
     Action (*Karma*) does not contradict ignorance (*Avidyā*); both can co-exist. Only Knowledge dispels Ignorance, as radiant sunlight destroys dense darkness.

2. **The Phenomenological Mechanism of Adhyāsa (Superimposition) (Verses 7–8)**:
   - The world appearance is perceived as real only so long as the non-dual substratum (*Brahman*) remains unrealized, exactly like:
     - The appearance of silver in mother-of-pearl (*Śukti-rajata-nyāya*).
     - The terrifying illusion of a serpent in a piece of rope (*Rajju-sarpa-nyāya*).
     - The blueness and concave dome projected upon the colorless sky.

3. **Anatomy of the Upādhis (Limiting Adjuncts) & 5 Kośas (Verses 11–15)**:
   - **Sthūla-śarīra (Gross Body)**: Born of the five gross elements (*Pañcīkṛta-pañca-mahābhūta*), subject to birth, growth, decay, and death.
   - **Sūkṣma-śarīra (Subtle Body)**: Composed of 17 factors (5 organs of perception, 5 organs of action, 5 prāṇas, mind, intellect).
   - **Kāraṇa-śarīra (Causal Body)**: Inexplicable ignorance (*Anādyanirvācyāvidyā*).
   - **Pañca-kośas (The 5 Sheaths)**: *Annamaya*, *Prāṇamaya*, *Manomaya*, *Vijñānamaya*, *Ānandamaya*. The Self is distinct from all five, like the pure grain inside husks of paddy.

4. **The Unattached Witness (*Sākṣī Caitanya*) (Verses 18–21)**:
   - The moon appears to move when clouds drift across the night sky; similarly, the motionless Ātman appears active due to the continuous fluctuations of the intellect and senses (*Citta-vṛttis*).

5. **Hermeneutics of Mahāvākyas (Verses 29–30)**:
   - Deconstructs *Tat Tvam Asi* through *Bhāga-tyāga-lakṣaṇā* (Discarding the contradictory finite attributes of the Jīva—mortality, ignorance—and the cosmic attributes of Īśvara—omniscience, creatorhood—to reveal the underlying invariant identity of Pure Consciousness).

6. **Jīvanmukti & Saccidānanda Realization (Verses 49–54, 68)**:
   - The enlightened sage lives in the world unattached, radiant, like water on a lotus leaf.
   - Reaches the supreme crescendo echoed in the *Nirvāṇaṣaṭkam*: चिदानन्दरूपः शिवोऽहम् शिवोऽहम् (*I am of the nature of Pure Consciousness and Bliss, I am Śiva*).

## 3. Dossier 02: Puruṣa Sūkta (Ṛgveda 10.90) — Cosmic Organicism & Sociological Deconstruction

### 3.1 Textual & Cosmological Structure
The *Puruṣa Sūkta* is the foundational cosmogonic hymn of the Vedic Saṃhitās. It represents the ancient transition from mythological polytheism to transcendental **Cosmic Organicism (Cosmopsychism)**.

1. **Infinite Pan-En-Theism (Verses 1–3)**:
   - *Text*: सहस्रशीर्षा पुरुषः सहस्राक्षः सहस्रपात्। स भूमिं विश्वतो वृत्वात्यतिष्ठद्दशाङ्गुलम्॥ (v. 1)
   - *Meaning*: The Cosmic Being has thousands of heads, eyes, and feet (symbolizing every sentient living creature). Pervading the material cosmos from all sides, He transcends it by ten fingers (*daśāṅgulam*—an idiom denoting transcendental infinity beyond spatial dimensions).
   - *Text*: पुरुष एवेदं सर्वं यद्भूतं यच्च भव्यम्। उतामृतत्वस्येशानो यदन्नेनातिरोहति॥ (v. 2)
   - *Meaning*: The Puruṣa alone is all that has been and all that will be. He is the sovereign master of immortality.
   - *Text*: पादोऽस्य विश्वा भूतानि त्रिपादस्यामृतं दिवि॥ (v. 3)
   - *Meaning*: All manifest beings constitute merely one-fourth (*Pāda*) of His reality; three-fourths remain immortal and transcendent in the luminous unmanifest realm.

2. **The Cosmic Yajña (Verses 6–10)**:
   - The creation of the material universe is modeled as a cosmic self-sacrifice (*Sarvahuta Yajña*). The Divine does not manufacture the world out of nothing (*ex nihilo*), nor from external dead matter; rather, the Divine offers Himself as the material and efficient cause (*Abhinna-nimittopādāna-kāraṇa*).

### 3.2 The Sociological Locus: Ṛgveda 10.90.12 Deconstruction
- *Text*: ब्राह्मणोऽस्य मुखमासीद् बाहू राजन्यः कृतः। ऊरू तदस्य यद्वैश्यः पद्भ्यां शूद्रो अजायत॥ (v. 12)
- *Literal Translation*: "The Brāhmaṇa was His mouth; the Rājanya (Kṣatriya) was made His arms; His thighs were the Vaiśya; from His feet the Śūdra was born."

#### A. The Colonial Weaponization Optic
- **Colonial Anthropologists (Herbert Risley, James Mill, John Muir)**: Interpreted this verse through Victorian class and racial hierarchies, claiming it proved that Indian society was intentionally created as an apartheid system where 80% of citizens are "degraded foot-creatures" born from an unclean bodily extremity.
- **Flaw**: Projects European physical bodily taboos (feet as dirty) onto a sacred Vedic cosmology that worships the feet of the Divine.

#### B. The Classical Vedic Organicism Optic (Macrocosm-Microcosm Isomorphism)
1. **The Single Indivisible Body (*Ekatva*)**:
   - The verse describes **one living body**, not four separate species. In any organism, the head cannot survive without the feet, nor can the feet survive without the head. An injury to the foot is registered instantly as pain in the brain.
2. **The Sacredness of the Feet (*Pratiṣṭhā*)**:
   - In Vedic ontology, the feet represent the **foundation, stability, and support (*Pratiṣṭhā*)** of the cosmos.
   - Proof from the exact same hymn (*RV 10.90.14*):
     *पद्भ्यां भूमिर्दिशः श्रोत्रात् तथा लोकाँ अकल्पयन्॥*  
     "From His feet the **Earth (Bhūmi)** was born, and from His ears the cardinal directions."
   - In Vedic religion, the Earth is revered as the Supreme Mother (*Mātā Bhūmiḥ Putro'haṃ Pṛthivyāḥ* - Atharvaveda 12.1.12). If the sacred Earth emanates from the feet of the Puruṣa, then by mathematical transitivity, the feet in Vedic thought are the locus of maternal, stabilizing sanctity, never of degradation or contempt.
3. **Cosmic Functional Subsystems**:
   - **Mukham (Mouth/Head)**: Intellectual, pedagogical, communicative subsystem (transmission of knowledge, speech, truth).
   - **Bāhū (Arms)**: Sovereign, kinetic, defensive subsystem (protection of society, military-industrial shielding, justice).
   - **Ūrū (Thighs/Torso)**: Metabolic, economic, wealth-circulating subsystem (agriculture, trade, manufacture, supply chains).
   - **Padbhyām (Feet/Base)**: Foundation, infrastructure, physical labor, grounding subsystem (material support upon which all other structures stand).

#### C. Human Psychological & Somatic Correspondence
- Every human individual possesses all four functional powers:
  - The capacity to learn, speak, and seek wisdom (*Brāhmaṇa function*).
  - The capacity to defend oneself, demonstrate courage, and establish boundaries (*Kṣatriya function*).
  - The capacity to produce, trade, budget, and sustain livelihoods (*Vaiśya function*).
  - The capacity to execute physical work, build infrastructure, and provide grounded support (*Śūdra function*).

#### D. The Historical Deviation (*Vikṛti*) vs Vedic Norm
- The historical petrification occurred when this **organic functional specialization (*Guṇa-Karma-Vibhāgaḥ* per Gītā 4.13)** was converted during later medieval periods into an unyielding, hereditary, endogamous hierarchy (*Janma-Jāti*).
- **Mīmāṃsā Jurisprudential Invalidation (Jaimini 1.3.3)**:  
  *विरोधे त्वनपेक्षं स्यादसति ह्यनुमानम्॥*
